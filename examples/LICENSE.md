# e2Unit examples — MIT License

The files in this folder are **example code**: a fixed-point library, its test suites and reference models, showing
how to write e2Unit tests. Copy them into your projects, adapt them, ship them in your products (commercial ones
included): no e2Unit license is needed for that. They are provided as is, test them on your own hardware.

The e2Unit PC tool itself is licensed separately, see [../LICENSE.md](../LICENSE.md).
